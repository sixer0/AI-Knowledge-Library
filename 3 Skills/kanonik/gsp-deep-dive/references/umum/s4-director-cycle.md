# S5 - Director Cycle (human stage only; the fleet prepares materials)

Purpose: hand the measured package to the Director and support his decision
cycle without ever stepping into it. There is no fleet gate at this stage; the
stage exists so the fleet knows exactly what to prepare and what never to do.
Identical for every object class.

## Execution checklist
1. The Director decides using his own vocabulary; outcome words belong to him
   alone. They appear in records of his decisions, never in fleet-authored
   output surfaces.
2. Prepare the meeting brief on request: the record surface plus the two-line
   unknown-and-route closers per section, nothing added.
3. Answer additional measurement requests: each new request opens a lane under
   the same offensive-search and SPK-002 discipline, feeding the same ledgers.
4. Counterparty answer cycle: answers re-enter claims-ledger.csv, claim
   statuses update, dimension cards recompute through the parser, and a delta
   note is issued showing what moved and why. No billing clocks: open items
   without answers stay dated named items, re-presented in every delta note.
   The fleet does not chase and does not infer from silence.
5. On a Director instruction to advance: term-sheet or proposal drafting
   starts on command. On a Director instruction to decline: a polite letter
   with the recorded reasons is prepared. Both are executed orders, not fleet
   initiatives.

## What the fleet never does here
- Interpret the Director's vocabulary into a fleet position.
- Fill `Director decision: ______` in any artifact, even after the decision is
  known verbally; the slot mirrors the record channel, not the conversation.
- Treat a decision as closing research: refresh triggers in S6 keep the record
  alive regardless of outcome.

## Common pitfalls
- Preparing the meeting brief with a summary sentence that ranks options. The
  brief orders sections as the record does; ranking is a decision shape.
- Recomputing cards by hand after a counterparty answer. Only the parser
  recomputes; hand arithmetic drifts from the frozen weights.
