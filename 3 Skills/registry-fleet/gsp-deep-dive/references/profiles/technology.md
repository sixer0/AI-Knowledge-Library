# Profile B2 - TECHNOLOGY / PRODUCT (thin instantiation of general defaults)

Status: initial instantiation per blueprint 02b section 2.2 (AD-3). Content
below is neutral instantiation of Part A defaults plus precedent provenance; it
creates no new substantive rules. Expansion happens only through the Decision
Log. Precedents: EKAS corpus work, BUKUV3 book-verification missions.

## B2.1 Trigger
The Director nominates one technology or product (named capability, platform,
or component) plus deadline. Nomination unit: one technology/product identity
(entity-match applies to the owning entity and the product name; product names
collide more often than company names). Tiering as general: T1 always, T2/T3
by user choice after the screen report. Folder shape:
`docs/<date>_dd-<technology>/`.

## B2.2 Lanes (all at equal full depth)
1. capability-vs-physics - what the technology can do against physical limits
   and independent benchmarks.
2. maturity-evidence - claimed vs demonstrated maturity (M1..M5 discipline).
3. integration-fit - fit into GSP lines and Indonesian operating conditions.

## B2.3 Mandatory questions
The seven general lane questions (s2-lane-dissection.md) applied per lane:
21 grid rows minimum in 02-lanes/lane-grid.csv (manifest
scripts/profiles/technology.json). Provenance of the question set: the general
five-x-seven bank of the vendor era reduced to its object-neutral core
(std-source section 3; EKAS RANTAI-JAMINAN evidence-chain practice).

## B2.4 Typical flags (above the general baseline four)
- maturity inflation: demo-stage capability presented as deployable
  (measured via the M2 self-report ceiling and third-party corroboration);
- dependency concentration: single supplier/cloud/government dependency in a
  named country;
- standards gap: no path to the certification the Indonesian buyer requires
  (BSSN, BIG, TKDN where applicable).

## B2.5 Typical artifacts
02-lanes/lane-grid.csv (general AA.3 schema), saturation/{capability,maturity,
integration}.md, query-log.csv, claims-ledger.csv (frozen schema), one-line
capability card per claim in the ledger. No maps gate defined. Gate manifest:
scripts/profiles/technology.json.

## B2.6 Presentation focus
Findings block first, then capability verdict surface (scores + labels),
maturity ladder with evidence trail, integration options into GSP lines,
known unknowns. Depth identical across lanes regardless of order.
