- reference-image protocol (3-4 exemplar board, assign role, instruksi multi-image Nano Banana) — HIGH; photographic art-direction block (kamera/lensa/cahaya/grade wajib pada hero) — MED; grounding + edit-loop (prefer Nano Banana grounding untuk fakta; koreksi via in-paint bukan reroll) — MED; keep v7.1 density caps (≤18 labels/≤10 numbers/≤6 zones + ~400-word ceiling, kini terkonfirmasi independen) — KEEP (Section 9).
