# Metodologi verifikasi laporan: 4 kriteria fungsional + PM-quality 6 checks

Verifikasi laporan GSP dijalankan dua lapis: **(1) verifier fungsional** — 4 kriteria: Intent Alignment (R1–R5), Completeness (9 section + AC-1..AC-30), Consistency (cross-check filesystem: 28/28 nilai exact), Safety (audit kredensial/injection/path); **(2) pm-verifier** — 6 checks PM-1..PM-6 (document completeness 26/26, structural quality 10/10, alignment 5/5, data integrity 28/28, actionability, edge cases 14/14). Kedua lapisan menulis pada file 01_verification.md dengan section terpisah. [R — 01_verification.md, hal. 24–33]
