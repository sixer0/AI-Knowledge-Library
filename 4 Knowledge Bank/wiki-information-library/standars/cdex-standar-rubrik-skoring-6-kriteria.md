# China Defence Expo — rubrik skoring vendor 6 kriteria berbobot (25/20/20/15/10/10)

Rubrik skoring vendor (01_synthesis_framework.md, Section 1): 6 kriteria berbobot (total 100%), tiap kriteria skor 1-10 -> skor berbobot -> composite.
1. Technical Capability 25% (spesifikasi, inovasi, sertifikasi GJB/ISO, R&D)
2. Reliability 20% (umur usaha, stabilitas finansial, karyawan, struktur kepemilikan, sengketa)
3. Export Status 20% (BIS Entity, Pentagon CMCL, sanksi EU, lisensi ekspor, risiko geopolitik)
4. Procurement Readiness 15% (sertifikasi GJB, lisensi produksi militer, dual-use, QMS)
5. Regional Coverage 10% (kehadiran lintas region, jaringan subsidiary)
6. Industry Context 10% (rujukan SIPRI/IISS/Jane's, kertas putih pertahanan China, posisi kompetitif)

Contoh: Technical 7, Reliability 5, Export 6, Procurement 5, Regional 5, Industry 6 -> composite 6,75 -> Tier 2.
