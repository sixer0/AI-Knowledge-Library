# Taksonomi Kategori Vendor China Expo (10 kategori)

Taksonomi klasifikasi vendor dari riset China Expo: definisi, kata kunci, dan panduan klasifikasi.

| Kategori | Definisi/Cakupan | Kata Kunci | Panduan Klasifikasi |
| Defence & Security | Military, homeland security, public safety, border security, tactical systems, protective solutions, command-and-control products. | defence, military, tactical, border, public safety, command center, C4ISR | Core offering directly supports defence, law enforcement, or national security operations. |
| Artificial Intelligence | AI/ML platforms, computer vision, language intelligence, autonomous analytics, decision support, AI-enabled security products. | AI, machine learning, computer vision, NLP, analytics, autonomous | AI is a product capability, not merely a marketing statement. |
| Cyber Security | Network, endpoint, cloud, application, identity, data, threat intelligence, security operations technologies. | SOC, SIEM, XDR, firewall, zero trust, IAM, threat intelligence | Products that protect digital systems, networks, identities, applications, or information. |
| Communication Interception & Warfare | Signals interception, lawful interception, spectrum monitoring, communications intelligence, jamming, tactical communication exploitation. | SIGINT, COMINT, lawful interception, spectrum monitoring, jamming, radio intelligence | Only when interception, monitoring, exploitation, or disruption of communications is explicitly supported. |
| Malware | Malware analysis, detection, reverse engineering, sandboxing, malicious-code intelligence, anti-malware research. | malware, sandbox, reverse engineering, malicious code, ransomware | Vendors specializing in malware research, detection, analysis, or response. |
| Electronic Warfare | Electronic support, electronic attack, electronic protection, radar countermeasures, RF detection, jamming, spectrum dominance. | EW, ESM, ECM, ECCM, RF detection, radar warning, jamming | Military-grade electromagnetic spectrum operations and countermeasure systems. |
| Surveillance & Intelligence | Video surveillance, ISR, OSINT, GEOINT, intelligence fusion, reconnaissance, biometrics, tracking, situational awareness. | OSINT, GEOINT, ISR, surveillance, reconnaissance, biometrics, tracking | Collection, monitoring, intelligence production, fusion, and operational awareness systems. |
| Digital Forensic | Collection, preservation, examination, recovery, analysis of evidence from computers, mobile devices, networks, cloud, multimedia. | digital forensic, mobile forensic, e-discovery, evidence recovery, forensic imaging | Legally defensible digital evidence acquisition or analysis. |
| Industrial/OT Security | Security for industrial control systems, SCADA, critical infrastructure, manufacturing, utilities, energy, transport, connected operational environments. | OT, ICS, SCADA, critical infrastructure, industrial cybersecurity, IIoT | Security technologies designed specifically for operational or industrial assets. |
| Social Media, E-Commerce, Content Creation, Online Market Insight | Social listening, digital media intelligence, e-commerce analytics, content intelligence, influencer monitoring, brand/market insight, online ecosystem analysis. | social listening, e-commerce intelligence, content analytics, market insight, media monitoring | Platforms analyzing public online content, marketplaces, audiences, narratives, brands, or digital behavior. |
