# Framework GSP orchestration: 10-fase lifecycle dengan phase-gate dan delegasi sub-agen

GSP orchestration adalah framework **10 fase** (identification → research → masterplan → implementation → verification → closure, dengan fase 1–4 dikelola controller P1–P4): setiap fase men-delegasikan pekerjaan ke sub-agen (request-translator, task-architect, data-collector, data-analyst, pm-writer, verifier, pm-verifier) dengan **phase-gate enforcement** dan artefak standar (01_translated, 02_structured, 03_analysis, 01_specs, 02_plan, 99_implementation_report, 01_verification, report, decisions).
