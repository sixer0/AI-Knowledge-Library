# Thermo Scientific Nicolet iS50 FTIR Spectrometer — spesifikasi teknis

## Ringkasan
Nicolet iS50 adalah spektrometer FTIR workstation dari Thermo Fisher Scientific (USA) yang ditawarkan ke Labinsen TNI AL. Sistem menggabungkan multi-tasking dan performa tinggi: ATR diamond built-in, automated beamsplitter exchanger (ABX), serta opsi modul Raman, NIR, TGA-IR dan GC-IR.

## Fakta & provenans
- Resolusi optik mid-IR: < 0,09 cm-1; jangkauan spektral standar 7800–350 cm-1; opsi optik CsI 6400–200 cm-1; multi-range 27.000–10 cm-1 [PENAWARAN_PT_GSP.pdf — lampiran spesifikasi produk, hal 2-? (lembar produk PS52301_E)]
- Signal-to-noise 1 menit scan: 65.000:1 tipikal, 55.000:1 terjamin; SNR 5 detik > 13.000:1 [lampiran]
- Interferometer Vectra dynamic alignment; sumber Polaris mid-IR long-lifetime + Tungsten-Halogen NIR/Vis; hingga 5 detektor terpasang [lampiran]
- ATR diamond built-in: rentang 5000–80 cm-1 (mid- sampai far-IR), down force 60 lbs, tray dapat dilepas [lampiran]
- ABX: hingga 3 beamsplitter, < 25 detik per exchange, diameter beamsplitter 52 mm [lampiran]
- Modul Raman iS50: laser 1064 nm diode, daya > 450 mW, spot < 60 micron, stage 5 micron steps, berat 7,6 kg [lampiran]
- Modul GC-IR: flow cell & transfer line 300 °C, light pipe 15 cm x 1 mm gold-coated, splitter 10:1 [lampiran]
- Dimensi base: 62,6 x 69,8 x 27,6 cm; berat base 60 kg (dengan ABX 64 kg) [lampiran]
- Interface USB 2.0; A/D 24-bit; OS Windows 10; OMNIC software; opsi ValPro & 21 CFR Part 11 [lampiran]
- Garansi: source/interferometer 10 tahun, laser 5 tahun, spektrometer 1 tahun [lampiran]
- Kelas produk: "For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures." [lampiran]
