# OZM FSKM 10 — BAM Friction Apparatus (uji sensitivitas gesekan)

## Ringkasan
FSKM 10 dari OZM Research adalah BAM Friction Apparatus untuk penentuan sensitivitas gesekan (friction sensitivity) bahan energetik: high explosives, propellants, pyrotechnics, primary explosives. Keunggulan utama: kontrol kecepatan plat presisi tinggi via step motor digital dan akurasi posisi terbaik; konstruksi stainless steel; rentang beban gesek lebar 0,1–360 N.

## Fakta & provenans
- Rentang friction load: 0,1–360 N; arm standar BAM-6A (6-posisi) + 9 pcs weights: 5–360 N; aksesori PEx-4: 0,5–360 N; arm ringan PEx-3A + PEx-14: 0,1–10 N [PDS_FSKM_10.pdf, hal 1-3]
- Standar kepatuhan: UN Manual of Tests and Criteria (New York, 2019) Test 3(b)(i); EN 13631-3:2004; STANAG 4487 (Friction Sensitivity Tests); Commission Regulation (EC) No 440/2008 Method A.14 [hal 1-2]
- Komponen standar: base+friction device (BFST-FD), loading arm BAM-6A (BFST-BAM6A), set weights 0,28–10,08 kg (BFST-BAMW), working table 60x120x80 cm anti-statik (BFST-T), sampling spoons 5/10/40 mm³ (BFH-SP-3) [hal 3]
- Konsumabel: porcelain pegs (400/200 pcs), porcelain plates double-sided (100 pcs) [hal 3]
- Data pengiriman: paket 760x1350x1120 mm; gross ±185 kg; HS 9031 80 38 [hal 3]
- Instalasi: ruang 650x1200x1300 mm; berat 76 kg; daya listrik 230 V/50 Hz atau 120 V/60 Hz, 500 W; exhaust lokal (opsi BFH-EPS Ex-proof suction) [hal 3]
